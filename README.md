### Problem Statement: Credit Risk Modeling

**Basic Question to be Resolved**:
How can we accurately predict the likelihood of a customer defaulting on a loan or credit payment? The goal is to develop a predictive model that identifies high-risk customers to minimize financial losses and improve decision-making.

**Perspective/Context**:
The financial industry faces significant challenges in managing credit risk. Identifying customers who are likely to default is critical for maintaining profitability and reducing non-performing loans. This problem is particularly relevant in the context of increasing economic uncertainty and changing consumer behavior.

**Decision Makers/Stakeholders**:
Key stakeholders include financial institutions, credit risk managers, loan officers, and regulatory bodies. These groups rely on accurate predictions to make informed decisions about loan approvals and risk management.

**Criteria for Success**:
Success will be measured by the accuracy and recall of the predictive model. Key metrics include:
- High precision and recall for identifying defaulters.
- Improved decision-making efficiency for loan approvals.

**Scope of Solution Space**:
The solution will focus on developing a machine learning model using historical customer data. The scope includes data preprocessing, feature engineering, model training, and evaluation. The solution will not include external data sources or manual credit assessments.

**Key Sources of Insights**:
Insights will be derived from historical customer data, including demographic information, credit history, and transaction patterns. Additional insights may be gathered from industry reports and best practices in credit risk modeling.
